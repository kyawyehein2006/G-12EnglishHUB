Listening and Speaking

🎧 A. Listen to the views of three people on the use of AI.

Complete the following sentences with the appropriate words.

အသံဖိုင်ကို အနည်းဆုံး ၂ ကြိမ် နားထောင်ရန် အကြံပြုပါသည်။ ပထမအကြိမ်တွင် အဓိကအကြောင်းအရာ၊ ဒုတိယအကြိမ်တွင် အသေးစိတ်အချက်များကို အာရုံစိုက်ပါ။
🔎 Listening Tip
၂ ကြိမ်နားထောင်ပြီး မရသေးသောအချက်များရှိမှသာ Tape Script ကို ဖွင့်ကြည့်ပါ။

Cherry (35 years old)

It'll be nice to have a domestic robot at home. I wish I had one. You know, I am a mother of three kids working as an accountant at a travel agency which is far from my place. Arriving at work in time is one of the biggest challenges for me. I need to finish all the household chores before I leave home. When I get back from the office, I need to prepare dinner for the family. I am absolutely exhausted afterwards. So, I want a domestic robot that can clean the house, do the laundry and prepare meals for us. Robots would make our lives comfortable and better.

Steve (55 years old)

Well, I know that the development of AI can make our lives easier and increase productivity. So far it has been useful for us in workplace or at home. Nowadays, in some parts of the world human workers are being replaced by AI bots because they can do what human workers can't. We cannot predict what will happen in the future. With advanced technology, the evolution of AI bots is rapidly changing our lives. Today, they are our friends, tomorrow they may become our enemies. Who knows?

Harry (15 years old)

Oh! I am not worried about the rapid emergence of AI. For us, ChatGPT is very useful. When I have written assignments, I sometimes use GPT 4 to have some ideas about what to write. No, I don't copy whatever it says. I just use it to get inspiration for my assignments. I don't think they will become a threat to us. They are useful to all people - young or old, at all time. I think AI has become a “can’t-live-without” technology. Am I right? A big thank to those who created AI.

A

Complete the sentences

A1
Cherry wants to have a ________ robot in her house to help her with her household chores.
Answer: domestic
A2
It is not easy for Cherry to arrive at her ________ in time.
Answer: work
A3
Cherry is too ________ to prepare dinner for her family when she gets back from work.
Answer: exhausted
A4
Steve believes that our lives have become ________ with advancements in AI.
Answer: easier
A5
According to Steve, AI bots are displacing ________ workers in some workplaces.
Answer: human
A6
Harry thinks AI is not a ________ to us but useful to people of all ages.
Answer: threat
B

True or False

B1
Cherry is a busy working mother.
Answer: True
B2
Steve is not aware that the use of AI can increase productivity.
Answer: False
B3
Steve hopes that with the advanced technology, AI robots will become more useful to humans in the future.
Answer: False
B4
Harry thinks the emergence of AI is very helpful to us.
Answer: True
B5
Harry uses all the ideas Chat GPT gives when he writes his assignments.
Answer: False

Speaking Practice

C. Listen to their views again. Which view do you agree with?

၎င်းတို့၏အမြင်များကို ထပ်မံနားထောင်ပြီး မည်သည့်အမြင်ကို သင်သဘောတူသနည်း။

D. Work with a partner. Compare and discuss your ideas.

မိတ်ဖက်တစ်ဦးနှင့်အတူ သင့်အတွေးအမြင်များကို နှိုင်းယှဉ်ဆွေးနွေးပါ။

E. Interview your partner using the questions below.

  1. Do you want a robot friend? Why or why not?
    စက်ရုပ်မိတ်ဆွေတစ်ဦးကို သင်လိုချင်ပါသလား။ ဘာကြောင့်လဲ။
  2. Do you think robots will become our friends one day? Why or why not?
    တစ်နေ့တွင် စက်ရုပ်များသည် ကျွန်ုပ်တို့၏မိတ်ဆွေများ ဖြစ်လာမည်ဟု သင်ထင်ပါသလား။
  3. What is something you want a robot to help you with? Why?
    စက်ရုပ်က သင့်အား မည်သည့်အရာတွင် ကူညီပေးစေချင်သနည်း။ ဘာကြောင့်လဲ။

F. Tell the class about your friend's views on a robot friend.

သင့်မိတ်ဆွေ၏ စက်ရုပ်မိတ်ဆွေအပေါ် အမြင်ကို အတန်းအား ပြောပြပါ။

2E

Discussion

A. Think about the following points and make some brief notes.

  1. the benefits of AI — AI ၏ အကျိုးကျေးဇူးများ
  2. some of the potential dangers of AI — AI ၏ ဖြစ်နိုင်ခြေရှိသော အန္တရာယ်အချို့
  3. how people are currently using it — လက်ရှိ လူများ AI ကို မည်သို့အသုံးပြုနေကြသနည်း

B. Work in pairs and share your notes. Ask and answer the questions. Ask for further information.

နှစ်ယောက်တစ်တွဲလုပ်ပြီး သင့်မှတ်စုများကို မျှဝေပါ။ မေးခွန်းများကို အပြန်အလှန်မေးဖြေပြီး ထပ်မံသိရှိလိုသည့် အချက်အလက်များကို မေးပါ။
