Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs, an American businessman who founded Apple Inc. and NeXT Inc., was born in San Fransisco, California, U.S. in 1955. Jobs was only 19 when he started his invention – the Apple I, which he called the logic board. You might be amazed to know where and how he invented it. Jobs and his former high school friend Stephen Wozniak designed it in his family garage with the money obtained by selling Jobs’s Volkswagen minibus and Wozniak’s programmable calculator. The personal computer, the Apple, appealed to a broad audience and gradually, Jobs became one of the first entrepreneurs. With Jobs’s encouragement, Wozniak designed an improved model, the Apple II, complete with a keyboard, and they arranged to have a sleek, molded plastic case manufactured to enclose the unit.

Jeff Bezos

Nowadays, people all over the world are getting used to shopping online and most of them are familiar with the name Amazon.com., an online shopping website. Its founder is Jeff Bezos, an American entrepreneur, and he was born in New Mexico in 1964. In 1995, at the age of 31, Bezos began developing the software for the site, working out of his garage with a handful of employees. Under his guidance, Amazon became the largest retailer on the World Wide Web and the model for Internet sales. Named after the South American River, Amazon sold its first book. Amazon.com is an online merchant of books and later of a wide variety of products. It is a vast Internet-based enterprise that sells books, music, movies, housewares, electronics, toys, and many other goods. With the attractive motto Spend less, Smile more! Amazon stays ahead of the pack and is one of the best online shopping websites.

When students have a quiet place to read, research and exchange ideas, they often develop habits that support independent learning. A successful library does not need expensive technology to be useful. Clear organization, suitable books and a welcoming atmosphere can make a significant difference. Volunteers can also help younger learners choose books and practise reading.
